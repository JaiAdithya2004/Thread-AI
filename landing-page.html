FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my data used to train AI?

No. We use enterprise AI endpoints that don't train on or retain your data, and you can bring your own model so nothing leaves your control.

Can my manager read my chats?

No. Conversations are private to each user and enforced at the database level. Admins manage access and see usage, never chat content.

Which tools can I connect?

Over 1,000, including Slack, Gmail, Google Docs, Notion, HubSpot, GitHub, and more, all via one-click OAuth.

How long does setup take?

Minutes. Connect a tool, and your brain starts building automatically.
