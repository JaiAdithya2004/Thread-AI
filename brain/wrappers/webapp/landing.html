FAQ

Common questions

How does Intellark connect to my apps?

Intellark uses secure OAuth — the same way you'd "Sign in with Google." No API keys to manage, no code to write. Once authorized, syncing is automatic.

Is my company's data kept private?

Absolutely. Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit, never shared with third parties, and never used to train AI models. Each workspace is fully isolated.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are up and running in under 10 minutes. Connect your first app, ask your first question — Intellark starts indexing immediately.

Which apps are supported?

Intellark supports 1,000+ apps including Gmail, Slack, Notion, Google Drive, GitHub, Linear, HubSpot, Jira, Confluence, Asana, Microsoft Teams, Salesforce, and many more.

Will my AI costs spiral out of control?

No — Intellark is built with token efficiency at its core. Our RAG pipeline retrieves only the top-K relevant chunks (not your entire knowledge base), trims context to fit your query, and deduplicates semantically similar passages before they ever reach the AI model. Most queries use a fraction of a full context window. You pay for precision, not volume.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data is yours. When you cancel, you can export everything and we permanently delete it from our servers within 30 days. No lock-in, no hostage data.

Can I control who sees what?

Yes. Intellark has granular role-based access control. Sensitive data can be restricted to specific teams or individuals, and admins have full visibility over access logs.
