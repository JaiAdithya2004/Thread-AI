How It Works

Ask anything.
Get answers with sources.

Thread retrieves the most relevant facts from your connected knowledge base and cites exactly where each answer came from.

Hybrid search under the hood

Combines semantic vector search with full-text search, then fuses the results - so both meaning and keywords are matched.

Every answer is cited

Thread shows which fact came from which source. No hallucination, no guessing - just answers you can verify.

Knowledge stays current

Facts are deduplicated automatically. When something changes, the old version is marked stale - not silently overwritten.
